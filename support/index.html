What is Sketch Fields?

Sketch Fields is an educational tool for learning electric and magnetic fields and the finite element method from simple sketches. Draw electrodes, conductors, coils and magnets, set voltages and currents, and pick materials. Then see the voltage, the electric and magnetic fields and their field lines in two dimensions, how current spreads through a conductor, and the capacitance, resistance, inductance and forces the drawing gives. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later, in English.

Which field types and models does it have?

Electrostatics, current flow (direct current) and magnetic fields. Each works on the section of a long part, per meter of depth or for a depth you type; electrostatics also works on a round part (axisymmetric) inside a closed outer electrode. In current flow a plane model can also be a thin sheet that carries current in its own plane. Every field is static: fields that change over time, such as alternating currents, are not part of Sketch Fields.

What is free, and what does Sketch Fields Pro add?

Free: open, run and explore every built-in example in every field type, and draw your own dielectrics in PTFE, FR-4 or air between and around the two plates of a capacitor, in electrostatics at the Coarse or Medium mesh. Projects, files, units and the help are free too. Sketch Fields Pro adds:

  • Current flow: conductors and terminals, current, resistance and power loss.
  • Magnetic fields: coils, magnets and iron, flux lines and inductance.
  • More electrodes, zero-potential and symmetry edges, and probes.
  • Axisymmetric models for round parts.
  • Every material, and your own materials.
  • Fine meshes for more detail.
  • Edit the examples.
  • Reports with the model settings and probe readings.

How much does Sketch Fields Pro cost?

In the United States, US$4.99 per month with a 7-day free trial, or US$49.99 per year with a 14-day free trial. Prices differ in other countries, and the free trial is for eligible accounts only. The app shows the price for your country before you confirm.

How do I turn on Sketch Fields Pro on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Menu > Sketch Fields Pro, and tap Restore purchases. Restore purchases is also in Menu > About Sketch Fields.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Sketch Fields Pro, and cancel. Subscribers can also use Manage subscription in Menu > About Sketch Fields. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Nothing you made is deleted. All your sketches and projects still open and run. Changing a design beyond the free features needs Sketch Fields Pro again.

The app says the subscription could not be checked.

Connect to the internet and try again. Sketch Fields Pro stays active without a connection, and a failed check never turns it off.

Where are my projects stored, and how do I move them to another device?

Projects are saved on your device and are included in iCloud or computer backups of the device. They are not shown in the Files app, and deleting the app deletes them. To keep a copy or move a project, use Menu > Projects and files > Save as a file. It saves a Sketch Fields file (.sketchfields.json) to Files, Mail, AirDrop, or another place you choose. On the other device, open that file with Sketch Fields from Files or Mail, or use Menu > Open a file.

How do I delete my data?

Delete sketches and projects in Menu > Projects and files, or delete the app to remove everything stored in it. The app never uploads your sketches or pictures. The Privacy Policy says how to ask us to delete any other information.

Does Sketch Fields work offline?

Yes. Drawing and the calculation run on your device and work without a connection. Buying and restoring a subscription need a connection.

Can I use inches?

Yes. Setup > View > Units switches lengths, areas and depths between SI (mm) and US customary units (in), in every value you type and read, the probes, the charts and the report. Voltages, currents, fields, capacitance, inductance and the other electric and magnetic values stay in SI in both settings. Magnet sellers often quote gauss: 1 T is 10,000 G. Files always store SI, so a sketch reads the same in either setting.

Where do the material values come from?

From published sources and makers’ data sheets, each named in the app’s material list (Setup > Materials), with the condition its source gives. Values worked out from a source’s own values, such as a magnet’s recoil permeability, are labeled “derived”. They are typical values; real materials vary with the grade, the supplier and the manufacturing process. Magnet values are at room temperature (20 °C). You can copy a material and enter your own values.

How do I put a probe at a single point?

Choose the Line tool and the Probe role, then tap the point twice, or tap it once and press Finish. A Probe or a Guide can sit at a single point. An Electrode or a Terminal needs a line or an area.

Why does the field at a sharp corner keep rising when I choose a finer mesh?

At a sharp corner and at the edge of a thin plate, the field is not finite in theory, so the values there depend on the mesh. Capacitances, charges and other totals do not. Read the field a little away from the point, or round the corner.

Can I draw with Apple Pencil?

Yes. Once you use Apple Pencil, only Apple Pencil draws, and your fingers pan and zoom. To draw with a finger again, turn off Menu > Draw with Apple Pencil only.

How accurate are the results?

Sketch Fields is for education and learning only. Its results are estimates from a simplified two-dimensional model, verified against published exact solutions and benchmark cases but not validated against measurements or tested in real-world scenarios. Do not use them to design, rate, or approve any real part or equipment, or for safety, medical, engineering, or compliance decisions, such as those on electrical safety, high voltage, or medical implants and devices. You use them at your own risk; to the extent the law allows, Journey On is not responsible for decisions based on them. The Sketch Fields Terms of Use list the model’s limits and the benchmark cases.

What does the app do with my information?

Your sketches, pictures, and results stay on your device; the app never uploads them. The app has no account and no ads, and it does not track you. The Privacy Policy has the details.
