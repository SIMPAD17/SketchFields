Sketch Fields pricing

Sketch Fields is free to download and to use with its free features. Sketch Fields Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, with a free trial for new subscribers.

Free and Sketch Fields Pro

Free

  • Run and explore every example, in every field type.
  • Draw your own dielectrics in PTFE, FR-4 or air between and around the plates of a capacitor, in electrostatics.
  • The capacitance, the charge, the energy, and the largest field.
  • Coarse and Medium meshes.
  • Projects, files, units, and the help.

Sketch Fields Pro adds

  • Current flow: conductors and terminals, current, resistance and power loss.
  • Magnetic fields: coils, magnets and iron, flux lines and inductance.
  • More electrodes, zero-potential and symmetry edges, and probes.
  • Axisymmetric models for round parts.
  • Every material, and your own materials.
  • Fine meshes for more detail.
  • Edit the examples.
  • Reports with the model settings and probe readings.

In the iPhone and iPad app

  • Yearly: US$49.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial.
  • Monthly: US$4.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

Black Friday to Cyber Monday: Sketch Fields Pro is half price. The code BLACKFRIDAY takes half off your first year, and BLACKFRIDAYMONTHLY half off your first 3 months. Until November 30; not for current subscribers.

Half off your first year Half off your first 3 months

These are the prices in the United States. In other countries the App Store shows the price in your currency, and it may include taxes. Apple bills the subscription to your Apple Account, and free trials are for eligible new subscribers. Refunds for purchases in the app are handled by Apple, as the Refund Policy says.

Canceling

You can cancel at any time. Sketch Fields Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and your sketches still open and run after it ends. Cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current period ends.
